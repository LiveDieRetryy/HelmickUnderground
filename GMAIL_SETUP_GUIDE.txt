================================================================================
GMAIL INTEGRATION SETUP GUIDE
================================================================================

How to Send Emails Directly from HelmickUnderground@gmail.com

This will allow you to send emails from the admin panel that appear to come 
from your Gmail account, and replies will go directly to your Gmail inbox.

================================================================================
STEP 1: ENABLE 2-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Log in with HelmickUnderground@gmail.com
3. Scroll to "How you sign in to Google"
4. Click "2-Step Verification"
5. Follow the prompts to enable 2FA (use your phone number or authenticator app)

NOTE: This is required before you can create an App Password.

================================================================================
STEP 2: CREATE GMAIL APP PASSWORD
================================================================================

1. After enabling 2FA, go back to: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Scroll to "How you sign in to Google"
3. Click "App passwords" (or "2-Step Verification" → "App passwords")
4. You may need to sign in again
5. In the "Select app" dropdown, choose "Mail"
6. In the "Select device" dropdown, choose "Other (Custom name)"
7. Type: "Helmick Underground Admin"
8. Click "Generate"
9. Google will show you a 16-character password like: "abcd efgh ijkl mnop"
10. **COPY THIS PASSWORD** - you can't see it again!

================================================================================
STEP 3: ADD TO VERCEL ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
================================================================================

1. Go to your Vercel dashboard: https://vercel.com/helmickunderground
2. Select your project
3. Go to "Settings" → "Environment Variables"
4. Add these THREE variables:

   Variable 1:
   * Key: GMAIL_USER
   * Value: HelmickUnderground@gmail.com
   * Environments: ✓ Production, ✓ Preview

   Variable 2:
   * Key: GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD
   * Value: (paste the 16-character password - remove spaces)
   * Environments: ✓ Production, ✓ Preview

   Variable 3:
   * Key: GMAIL_FROM_NAME
   * Value: Helmick Underground
   * Environments: ✓ Production, ✓ Preview

5. Click "Save" for each variable
6. Redeploy your site (or wait for next deployment)

================================================================================
SECURITY & BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================

✅ App Passwords are secure:
   - They're specific to one app/device
   - You can revoke them anytime without changing your main password
   - They don't give access to your full Google account

✅ Keep them private:
   - Never share your App Password
   - Don't commit it to Git (it's in environment variables, not code)
   - If compromised, revoke it and generate a new one

✅ Sending limits:
   - Gmail allows ~500 emails per day
   - Perfect for targeted B2B outreach
   - Avoid mass mailing - focus on personalized messages

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: "Invalid credentials" error
Solution: 
- Make sure you removed all spaces from the App Password
- Verify the email address is correct
- Try generating a new App Password

Problem: "Less secure app access" error
Solution:
- This shouldn't happen with App Passwords
- Make sure you're using the App Password, not your regular password

Problem: Emails not sending
Solution:
- Check Vercel environment variables are set correctly
- Check your Gmail "Sent" folder to verify
- Check spam/junk if testing to your own email

Problem: Replies not coming through
Solution:
- Replies should automatically go to HelmickUnderground@gmail.com
- Check your Gmail inbox and spam folder
- Verify the "Reply-To" header is set correctly
